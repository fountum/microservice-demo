bus_api

This system collects ridership data from busses servicing a large metropolitan area to analyze. Each bus collects data about how what stop commuters are boarding the bus and fuel consumed.

The system receives batches every 30 minutes to an hour while the busses are in service. This city has a large network of busses and high population, meaning that up to 1000 batches of both types may be received at once. 

The data stores will enable:
1. Tracking of the cost-effectiveness of busses on different routes (ex. cost of fuel vs revenue from passengers)
2. Monitoring which areas are being over/underserviced (in general or at specific times) 

Users of the system are transit coordinators and city planners.
